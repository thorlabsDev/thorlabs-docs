No business- and legal-approved privacy notice for ThorNode's current Discord sign-in, registered-wallet, payment, support, and infrastructure workflows has been published in this documentation.

<Warning>
  This page is a status notice, not a privacy notice. It does not state what
  personal data is collected, why it is used, how long it is retained, where it
  is processed, who receives it, or which privacy rights apply.
</Warning>

## Before you provide personal data

1. Contact [support@thornode.io](mailto:support@thornode.io) for the approved notice applicable to the exact workflow you intend to use.
2. Confirm that the notice is current and covers that workflow.
3. Provide only the information required through the approved channel.

The previous ThorNode Cloud privacy policy described a different service and is not presented as the notice for current ThorNode products.

For a privacy request, describe the request and provide only the minimum account or transaction identifiers support asks for. Never send a wallet seed phrase, private key, endpoint token, replay key, Bifrost credential, or credential-bearing URL.
