---
title: "Privacy notice"
description: "Request the privacy notice for ThorNode account, wallet, payment, and support workflows."
---

The current ThorNode privacy notice is not hosted in this documentation. Contact [support@thornode.io](mailto:support@thornode.io) to request it before providing personal data through Discord sign-in, wallet registration, payments, or support.

For a privacy request, describe what you need and share only the minimum account or transaction identifiers requested by support. Never send a wallet seed phrase, private key, endpoint token, replay key, Bifrost route URL, or other secret.
