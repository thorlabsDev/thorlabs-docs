ThorNode's current terms, privacy notice, and refund policy are not hosted in this documentation. Contact [support@thornode.io](mailto:support@thornode.io) to request the document that applies to the product or payment flow you intend to use.

<Warning>
  Review the applicable document before purchasing a service or providing
  personal data.
</Warning>

| Document                         | Request it when                                                         |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Terms of service](/legal/terms) | You need the terms for a ThorNode product or paid service.              |
| [Privacy notice](/legal/privacy) | You want to know how account, wallet, payment, or support data is used. |
| [Refund policy](/legal/refunds)  | You want to understand refund eligibility or submit a refund request.   |

Payment recovery is separate from a refund request. If a payment was signed but access did not update, keep the transaction signature and follow [payment recovery troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting#payment-is-signed-but-access-is-not-updated). Do not pay twice.

Never send a wallet seed phrase, private key, endpoint token, replay key, Bifrost route URL, or other secret when requesting a legal document.
