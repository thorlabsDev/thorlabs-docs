The previous ThorNode Cloud documents described a different hosting and subscription service. They are not presented as the current policies for ThorNode's present products.

## Separate operational help from legal policy

Documentation can help recover a signed payment whose access state has not updated. That operational recovery does not create purchase rights or approve a refund. Use [payment recovery troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting#payment-is-signed-but-access-is-not-updated) for an unresolved transaction and the [refund status page](/legal/refunds) for a refund-policy request.

Do not send wallet seed phrases, private keys, pass tokens, endpoint URLs, or payment secrets when requesting legal documents. Follow the [safe support checklist](/reference/troubleshooting#contact-support-safely).
