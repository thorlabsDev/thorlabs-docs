<Warning>
  This page is not a refund policy and does not promise eligibility. A business-
  and legal-approved policy for current rental, renewal, and other paid flows
  has not yet been published in this documentation.
</Warning>

The previous ThorNode Cloud refund policy covered a different service and is not represented here as the policy for current ThorNode purchases. Before paying, contact [support@thornode.io](mailto:support@thornode.io) to obtain the applicable approved refund terms.

For an already submitted payment, preserve the public wallet address, transaction signature, approximate payment time, and any recovery or support code the dashboard displays. Do not pay twice while a transaction is being verified, and never send a seed phrase or private key.
