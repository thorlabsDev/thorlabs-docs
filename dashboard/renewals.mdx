---
title: "Renewals"
description: "Extend an eligible owned ThorNode pass with a registered Solana wallet and the live USDC payment flow."
---

The renewal flow extends the access period of an eligible pass NFT that you own. It does not renew rentals and does not change the pass tier.

## Eligibility

A pass can appear in **Renew** when it is an eligible owned NFT associated with one of your registered Solana wallets.

The following are not renewed through this flow:

- Direct rental access
- Honorary passes
- Gold passes
- Any pass the live dashboard marks as ineligible or non-renewable

Honorary and Gold passes are excluded because they do not use the standard paid renewal lifecycle.

<Note>
  Renewal availability and deadlines are state-dependent. Always follow the
  eligibility label, expiration time, countdown, and instructions shown for that
  pass in the dashboard.
</Note>

## Renew an owned pass

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the owning wallet">
    Sign in with Discord, open **Wallets**, and confirm that the wallet holding the NFT is registered. Connect that same wallet before payment.
  </Step>

<Step title="Select the pass">
  Open **Renew** and choose an eligible owned pass. Rentals and excluded pass
  types will not be offered as renewable items.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose a duration">
  Select **1**, **3**, **6**, or **12 months** from the renewal options offered
  for the pass.
</Step>

<Step title="Review the live terms">
  Confirm the pass, duration, USDC asset, amount, resulting expiration, and
  connected registered wallet shown by the dashboard.
</Step>

<Step title="Approve once and wait">
  Sign the payment and wait for verification. Do not submit a second payment
  while the first transaction is pending.
</Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the new expiration">
    The renewal is complete only when the dashboard reports success and shows the updated expiration for the pass.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not use a remembered deadline or an old policy page to time a renewal. Pass
  state can change; the on-screen countdown and actions are the source of truth.
</Warning>

## If the pass is missing

Check the following before contacting support:

1. You are signed in with the Discord account that has the owning wallet registered.
2. Your wallet extension is connected to the full address shown in **Wallets**.
3. The NFT is still held by that address.
4. The dashboard does not label the pass as rental, Honorary, Gold, or otherwise ineligible.
5. You are following the current on-screen renewal window and deadline.

## If payment is pending

Do not pay again. Save the transaction signature. If the current results screen offers a retry for that signature, use that action instead of starting a second payment. Contact [ThorNode support](mailto:support@thornode.io) if the renewal still does not resolve.

Never send support your seed phrase, private key, or endpoint credentials.
