---
title: "Direct rentals"
description: "Purchase time-limited ThorNode access when the dashboard direct-rental catalog is enabled, and recover a pending USDC payment safely."
---

When the direct-rental rollout is enabled for your account and environment, it provides time-limited ThorNode access without requiring you to own a pass NFT. The dashboard catalog determines which tiers, durations, and USDC amounts are currently available.

<Note>
  Open the dashboard from [thornode.io](https://www.thornode.io). Do not rely on
  an old price table or bookmarked checkout address. If **Rent** does not show a
  catalog, direct rental is not currently available to you.
</Note>

## Before you rent

- Sign in with Discord.
- Register the Solana wallet that will make the payment.
- Connect that same registered wallet in the rental flow.
- Review the live tier, duration, USDC asset, amount, and expiration information.

The connected wallet must match one of the wallet addresses registered to your dashboard account.

## Purchase a rental

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rent">
    If **Rent** is available, open it and wait for the current catalog to load.
  </Step>

<Step title="Choose a live offer">
  Select a tier and duration from the options currently displayed. If an option
  is absent, it is not available through the direct rental flow at that time.
</Step>

<Step title="Review the payment">
  Verify the registered wallet, USDC asset, exact amount, rental duration, and
  resulting access before signing.
</Step>

<Step title="Approve once">
  Approve the payment in your wallet, then keep the dashboard open while it
  submits and verifies the transaction.
</Step>

  <Step title="Wait for activation">
    Continue only after the dashboard marks the rental active. It will then appear as an access source in the dashboard and eligible endpoint product views.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  After you approve or submit a payment, do not pay again because the screen is
  slow, refreshed, or still verifying. A second payment can create a duplicate
  charge.
</Warning>

## Recover a pending payment

The dashboard preserves payment progress so a submitted transaction can be reconciled without paying twice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the current state">
    Return to the same rental flow and allow it to resume verification. Do not start a new purchase.
  </Step>

<Step title="Keep the recovery details">
  Copy the transaction signature and any recovery or support code shown by the
  dashboard. These identifiers are not wallet private keys, but you should still
  share them only with official support.
</Step>

  <Step title="Escalate without repaying">
    If the state remains pending, contact [ThorNode support](mailto:support@thornode.io) with the public paying wallet, transaction signature, recovery or support code, and approximate payment time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Support can use those details to find the submitted payment. Never send your seed phrase, private key, or endpoint token.

## After activation

Open **Endpoints**, select the rental, and create credentials for its available products and locations. The dashboard shows the exact expiration time and current entitlement.

Direct rentals do not appear in the owned-pass renewal flow. To continue after expiration, use only the rental options currently offered by the dashboard.

The pass-holder [vendor marketplace](/dashboard/vendor-marketplace) is a separate SOL-priced checkout-link flow. Do not treat a vendor offer as a direct USDC rental.
