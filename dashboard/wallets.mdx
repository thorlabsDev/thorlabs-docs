---
title: "Wallets"
description: "Register up to three Solana wallets for pass discovery, rentals, and renewals."
---

Registering a wallet proves that you control a Solana address and associates it with your Discord-authenticated dashboard account. ThorNode uses registered wallets to find eligible owned passes and authorize supported payments.

<Note>You can register up to three wallets per dashboard account.</Note>

## Register a wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the dashboard from [thornode.io](https://www.thornode.io) and sign in with Discord.
  </Step>

<Step title="Connect the intended wallet">
  Open **Wallets**, start the registration flow, and select the Solana address
  that owns your pass or will be used for a payment.
</Step>

<Step title="Review the message">
  Confirm that the address and dashboard account are correct. Registration uses
  a signed message to prove control of the address.
</Step>

  <Step title="Sign and confirm">
    Approve the message in your wallet. Wait until the address appears in the registered wallet list before continuing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  ThorNode never needs your seed phrase or private key. A wallet-registration
  prompt should not ask you to transfer assets. Reject any unexpected request
  and reopen the flow from the official dashboard.
</Warning>

## Use a wallet for payment

The wallet connected at payment time must be registered to your dashboard account. Before starting a rental or renewal:

1. Check that the connected address matches the address shown in **Wallets**.
2. Confirm that the wallet can use the USDC asset and network displayed by the dashboard.
3. Review the live amount and payment details before signing.

Switching accounts in a wallet extension can silently change the connected address. Recheck it on every payment confirmation.

## Remove a wallet

Open **Wallets**, choose the address, and follow the signed removal flow. Removing a wallet disconnects the address from your dashboard account; it does not move or destroy assets held by that address.

Before removal, make sure you will not lose dashboard access to a pass that is held only by that wallet. You can register the address again later if you still control it and have not reached the wallet limit.

## Troubleshooting

### The pass does not appear

- Confirm that its owner address is registered under the Discord account you are currently using.
- Confirm that your wallet extension is connected to the expected Solana address.
- Refresh after registration has completed.
- Check the dashboard for the pass's current eligibility and state.

### The payment screen rejects the wallet

The connected address may not match a registered address. Return to **Wallets**, compare the full address, then reconnect the correct account before retrying.

### The registration limit is reached

Remove a wallet you no longer use, then register the new address. Do not remove the only wallet associated with an access source you still need.

If a signature completed but the dashboard does not update, do not share the signature message or any secret material publicly. Contact [ThorNode support](mailto:support@thornode.io) with your Discord account and public wallet address.
