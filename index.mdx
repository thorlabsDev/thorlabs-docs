## Start in the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login with Discord">
    Open the dashboard from [thornode.io](https://www.thornode.io) and
    authenticate with the Discord account associated with your ThorNode access.
    If the website's dashboard link is unavailable, contact support instead of
    guessing a hostname from an old bookmark.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an access">
    Use an active owned pass or rental. Product tabs can remain visible even
    when an access is not eligible; enabled controls, locations, and current
    backend responses establish availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create credentials">
    Generate the token or product key you need, then copy its connection details
    directly from the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integrate and monitor">
    Keep credentials server-side, test the connection, and monitor current usage
    and limits in the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Locations, prices, limits, routes, and product availability can change. The
  authenticated dashboard and its live backend responses are authoritative.
</Note>

## Choose a product

| Need                                  | Product                                                | Transport     |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------- |
| Solana requests and subscriptions     | [ThorEdge RPC](/products/thoredge-rpc)                 | HTTPS and WSS |
| Standard Geyser-compatible streams    | [Yellowstone gRPC](/products/yellowstone-grpc)         | gRPC          |
| Decoded shred firehose                | [Pulse](/products/pulse)                               | QUIC          |
| Live raw shreds                       | [ShredStream](/products/shredstream)                   | UDP           |
| Historical shred delivery             | [ShredReplay](/products/shredreplay)                   | UDP           |
| Provider-aware transaction submission | [Private transactions](/products/private-transactions) | HTTPS         |
